Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

MyTutBuddy ("we", "us") provides free interactive Maths worksheets at this website. This page explains what information is, and isn't, collected when you use it.

What we currently collect

MyTutBuddy does not require an account, and does not currently collect, store, or share any personal information. Worksheets run entirely in your web browser — your answers, scores, and progress are not sent to or stored on any server. When you close the page or click "Try Again", that information is simply gone.

Cookies

This site does not currently use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

If we add features in the future that use cookies — for example, website analytics to understand which worksheets are popular, or advertising through a provider such as Google AdSense — we will update this page first to describe exactly what is collected and, where required by law, ask for your consent before anything is set.

Children's privacy

Many visitors to MyTutBuddy are likely to be school-age students. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. If we ever introduce features that could involve personal data (such as optional accounts), we will design them with additional care and safeguards for younger users, in line with applicable children's privacy laws.

Third-party links

This site does not currently link out to third-party services. If that changes, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

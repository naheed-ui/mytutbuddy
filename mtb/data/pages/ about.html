About MyTutBuddy

MyTutBuddy makes Maths practice interactive. Instead of printing out a worksheet or filling in a PDF, students work through real worksheets directly in the browser — typing answers, choosing multiple-choice options, matching pairs, and getting an instant score with clear feedback on what to review.

What we cover

Right now, MyTutBuddy focuses entirely on Maths, from Grade 6 through GCSE, A-Level, and SAT — covering topics like Algebra, Fractions, Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics, and Probability. We're steadily adding more worksheets across these grades and topics.

Why interactive, not PDF

A PDF worksheet still needs printing, or a separate app to mark it. MyTutBuddy worksheets mark themselves instantly, so students get feedback while the question is still fresh in their mind — and teachers or parents don't need to check answers by hand.
